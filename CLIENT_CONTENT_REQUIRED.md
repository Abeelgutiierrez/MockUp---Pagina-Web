# Client content required before production

- Official company description and legal entity information
- Founding year and verified company statistics
- Manufacturing partner information and approved facility photography
- Markets, export regions and approved customer logos
- Certifications, compliance credentials and verification documents
- Sustainability policies, commitments and metrics
- Confirmed materials, constructions and product catalogue
- Final brand assets, photography and social links
- Confirmation of address, phone and email
- Privacy policy, cookie policy and website terms

All quantitative claims, certifications and market coverage remain intentionally absent from this concept until supplied and approved by Fashion Texa.
